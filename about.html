About LearnCodeVisually

LearnCodeVisually is a free learning platform designed to help students and self-directed learners understand computer programming through visualization. Rather than memorizing code, learners can inspect examples, explore simulations, and try exercises to understand what programs do.

What can you learn?

Lessons introduce topics such as variables, selections, loops, arrays, functions, and other programming concepts through examples in Python, C, C++, Java, JavaScript and C#. The website includes quizzes, exercises and multilingual explanations.

Learning method

  1. Read the explanation of a programming concept.
  2. Make a prediction about the next program state or output.
  3. Step through the visualization and compare your prediction.
  4. Try the related exercise or quiz and revisit any mistakes.

Free educational access

Our goal is to make programming concepts easier to understand across different backgrounds and languages. Core lessons and learning visualizations are free to access. Future optional sponsorship or advertising may help maintain the platform.
